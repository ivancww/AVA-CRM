# Payout calculation limits

The Coverage view can display a reviewed scenario for independently payable lump sums or a documented shared-maximum relationship. It does not imply that face amounts add automatically. Each comparison requires a person, event, criteria, date, currency, source and user review; changes to referenced Benefits invalidate its stored snapshot.

General reducing-sum-assured calculations are not implemented. A future focused release requires the full authoritative formula, policy start and anniversary conventions, eligibility and caps, and verified customer-specific inputs. Medical reimbursement is separate: a claim calculation requires actual eligible expenses, benefit restrictions and limits, deductibles and other insurer payments, and the applicable contractual coordination rules. Without these inputs no claim total is presented. Product-name matching alone supplies neither formula nor entitlement.

AI-extracted excerpts and page references must be checked against the original contract; software cannot authenticate an excerpt merely by receiving it. Cross-policy comparisons are explicitly reviewed by the local user, who is not identified by an authenticated account in this workflow.
